About Me

About Captain Amany

I'm Captain Amany, a 2025 Physical Education graduate with more than 2 years of experience in fitness training.

I specialize in helping women build stronger, healthier, and more confident versions of themselves through structured and personalized fitness programs.

My approach combines training, nutrition guidance, progress tracking, and continuous follow-up. Every plan is built around the client's individual goals, body measurements, lifestyle, daily routine, and current fitness level.

Whether the goal is fat loss, muscle gain, bulking, body transformation, or improving overall fitness, I focus on creating a practical plan that can be followed consistently while working toward clear and sustainable progress.
